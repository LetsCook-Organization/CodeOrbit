How your progress score works

Your progress score goes from 0 to 100. It adds up four parts, all worked out from what you have saved in CodeOrbit:

1. Studying often (up to 30) 0 / 30

How many of the last 7 days you studied on.

2. Weekly goal (up to 30) 0 / 30

Hours in the last 7 days compared with your weekly goal in Settings.

3. Projects (up to 20) 0 / 20

How far along your projects are, on average.

4. Skills (up to 20) 0 / 20

How far along your skills are, on average.
